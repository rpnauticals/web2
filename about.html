Who We Are

Guidance built by people who've walked the same path.

RP Nauticals is a Merchant Navy education and career guidance institute based in New Delhi, India. We help aspiring seafarers navigate one of the most rewarding — and most confusing — career paths available to Indian students: a life at sea.

Our founders and mentors have seen firsthand how many talented students lose their way simply because they didn't have the right guidance at the right time. RP Nauticals was built to close that gap — offering honest counselling, structured exam preparation, and real support through every stage of the journey, from choosing the right entrance exam to stepping onto your first ship.

We believe in integrity, transparency, and student-first guidance. We don't just prepare you for exams — we prepare you for a career.

Our Story

Built by seafarers, for future seafarers

RP Nauticals began with a simple, uncomfortable observation: most students entering the Merchant Navy pipeline in India were being guided by people who had never actually stepped onto a ship. Brochures promised the world; reality on the deck was very different. We started RP Nauticals to close that gap — to be the guide our own founders wish they'd had before their first voyage.

Today, we're a small, focused team of ex-mariners, faculty and support specialists working out of Dwarka, New Delhi. Every student who joins us gets a personal mentor, a written career map, and one point of contact through admission, training and placement. No handoffs, no lost files, no vanishing WhatsApp numbers.

Our Mission

To make an honest, structured Merchant Navy career genuinely accessible to every aspiring Indian seafarer — regardless of city, coaching budget or family background.

Our Vision

To become India's most trusted name in Merchant Navy career guidance — the first place a student and their family turn to before making one of the biggest decisions of their lives.
