Comprehensive coaching for the Indian Maritime University Common Entrance Test — India's most widely accepted Merchant Navy entrance.

Overview

About the course

IMU CET is the gateway to 30+ maritime institutes across India, covering DNS, B.Tech Marine, GME, and B.Sc Nautical Science programs. Our structured coaching combines strong fundamentals in Physics, Chemistry, Maths and English with disciplined mock testing and personal doubt-solving.

Eligibility

Who can apply

  • 10+2 with PCM (Physics, Chemistry, Maths)
  • Minimum 60% aggregate (varies by institute)
  • Age 17-25 years at time of admission
  • Medical fitness as per DG Shipping standards
  • Unmarried candidates for most cadet programs
Syllabus

What you'll study

Physics

Mechanics, Optics, Electricity, Modern Physics — problem-solving focus.

Chemistry

Physical, Organic and Inorganic — 10+2 board-level depth.

Mathematics

Algebra, Calculus, Trigonometry, Vectors, Probability.

English

Grammar, comprehension, vocabulary, sentence correction.

General Aptitude

Reasoning, data interpretation and general awareness.
