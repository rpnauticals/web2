Frequently Asked Questions

Home/FAQs

250+ answers on careers, admission, eligibility, training, placement, documentation and myths — searchable and organised.

How to build a career in Merchant Navy after 12th?+

Join a DG Shipping-approved course (DNS, B.Tech Marine, or GP Rating) after 12th with PCM, clear IMU CET, complete training, then start sailing as a cadet.

What is Merchant Navy exactly?+

It's the fleet of commercial ships that transport cargo/passengers globally — different from Navy (which is military/defense).

What is the difference between Navy and Merchant Navy?+

Navy is a defense force protecting the nation; Merchant Navy is a commercial industry transporting goods worldwide. Completely different career paths.

What is a Deck Cadet?+

A trainee officer on the navigation side — learns ship handling, cargo operations, and navigation, eventually becoming Captain.

What is an Engine Cadet?+

A trainee officer on the technical side — learns machinery, engines, and propulsion systems, eventually becoming Chief Engineer.

Which is better, Deck or Engine side?+

Both have similar pay and growth; choice depends on interest — Deck involves navigation/paperwork, Engine involves machinery/technical work.

What is DNS course?+

Diploma in Nautical Science — a 1-year pre-sea course leading to Deck Cadet position, followed by sea time and further officer exams.

What is GME (Graduate Marine Engineer)?+

A 1-year pre-sea engineering course for Engine side, followed by sea time and further officer-level exams.

What is B.Tech Marine Engineering?+

A 4-year engineering degree course combining academics with sea training, leading to Engine officer roles.

DNS vs B.Tech Marine — which is better?+

DNS is shorter and Deck-focused; B.Tech Marine is longer, Engine-focused, and gives a formal engineering degree.

What is GP Rating?+

General Purpose Rating — a shorter course (6 months-1 year) for crew-level (non-officer) roles, open to students without PCM.

Can I become Captain directly after training?+

No — you start as Cadet, then progress through ranks (2nd Officer, Chief Officer, Captain) over years of sea experience and exams.

How many years to become a Captain/Chief Engineer?+

Typically 8-10+ years of sea service and passing rank-wise competency exams (COC exams).

Is Merchant Navy a good career option?+

Yes — high salary, tax benefits (for NRIs), international exposure, and fast career growth compared to many shore jobs, though it involves time away from home.

What subjects are needed in 12th for Merchant Navy?+

PCM (Physics, Chemistry, Maths) is mandatory for Deck/Engine officer courses; not required for GP Rating.

Is Merchant Navy only for boys?+

No, girls can join too — the industry is actively encouraging more female officers in recent years.

What is sea time?+

The mandatory duration a cadet/officer must spend sailing onboard ships to gain experience and qualify for the next rank/exam.

What is COC (Certificate of Competency)?+

An official certification required to move up ranks (like 2nd Officer, Chief Officer, Captain), earned through exams conducted by DG Shipping.

Can I switch from Deck to Engine side later?+

No, once you choose Deck or Engine, you generally stay on that path — switching requires starting a fresh course.

What is the retirement age in Merchant Navy?+

There's no fixed retirement age at sea, but most officers move to shore-based roles by their 50s-60s due to physical demands.

What is the eligibility for Merchant Navy admission?+

12th pass with PCM (min 60% usually), age typically 17-25, and medical fitness — varies slightly by institute/course.

What entrance exam is required for Merchant Navy?+

IMU CET (Indian Maritime University Common Entrance Test) is the most widely accepted; some private institutes have their own tests too.

What is IMU CET?+

A national-level entrance exam for admission into Merchant Navy courses across IMU-affiliated institutes in India.

When does IMU CET happen?+

Usually conducted twice a year (check the official IMU website for current dates, as they can vary yearly).

What is the syllabus for IMU CET?+

Physics, Chemistry, Maths, English, and General Awareness — based on 10+2 level concepts.

Can I get admission without IMU CET?+

Some private institutes allow direct admission without IMU CET, but choosing IMU CET-affiliated institutes is generally more reliable.

What is the age limit for Merchant Navy admission?+

Typically 17-25 years for cadet-level courses; slight variations exist by institute and specific course.

How much does a Merchant Navy course cost?+

₹3-5 lakh for GP Rating; ₹8-15 lakh+ for DNS/B.Tech Marine, depending on govt vs private institute.

Are there government colleges for Merchant Navy?+

Yes — like IMU campuses, TS Chanakya, and other govt-affiliated institutes, generally more affordable than private ones.

What are the top Merchant Navy colleges in India?+

IMU Chennai, TS Chanakya (Navi Mumbai), Marine Engineering and Research Institute (Kolkata/Mumbai), and Anglo Eastern Maritime Academy, among others.

What documents are needed for admission?+

10th/12th marksheets, ID proof, medical fitness certificate, passport (or application proof), and category certificates if applicable.

Can I get an education loan for Merchant Navy courses?+

Yes, most banks offer education loans for DG Shipping-approved Merchant Navy courses, similar to other professional courses.

Is scholarship available for Merchant Navy courses?+

Some institutes and government schemes offer merit-based or category-based scholarships — check with specific colleges directly.

What is the minimum percentage required in 12th?+

Usually 50-60% in PCM, though it varies by institute — some private colleges have lower cutoffs.

Can I apply directly after 12th board results?+

Yes, admissions typically open right after 12th board results align with IMU CET application cycles.

Is there an interview during admission?+

Yes, many institutes conduct a personal interview and sometimes a group discussion along with the entrance exam.

Can I apply to multiple Merchant Navy colleges?+

Yes, through IMU CET counseling you can list multiple preferred institutes based on your rank/score.

What is the admission process after IMU CET?+

Qualify IMU CET, participate in counseling/choice filling, get seat allotment based on rank, complete document verification, then confirm admission.

Can a dropper (gap year student) apply for Merchant Navy?+

Yes, gap years are usually acceptable as long as you meet the age limit and eligibility criteria at the time of application.

Is there a medical test required before admission?+

Yes, a preliminary medical fitness check is often required before or right after admission, followed by a detailed pre-employment medical exam later.

What is the medical fitness criteria for Merchant Navy?+

Good eyesight, normal color vision (for deck), healthy heart/lungs, no major chronic illness, and overall physical fitness — verified through a DG Shipping-approved medical exam.

Can a colorblind person join Merchant Navy?+

No, for Deck officer roles — normal color vision is mandatory to read navigation lights and signals. Engine side has slightly more flexibility, but severe cases are still disqualified.

What is the height requirement for Merchant Navy?+

Usually minimum 150-157 cm depending on the institute/course — not extremely strict, but proportionate weight and fitness matter more.

What is the weight requirement for Merchant Navy?+

No fixed number, but BMI should be in a healthy range — being significantly overweight or underweight can affect medical fitness clearance.

Can someone with weak eyesight join Merchant Navy?+

Yes, if it's correctable with glasses/contact lenses within acceptable limits. Uncorrected vision beyond certain limits may disqualify for some ranks.

Is 20/20 vision mandatory?+

Not exactly 20/20, but vision must meet DG Shipping standards, correctable with lenses. Each course/rank has slightly different limits.

Can I join Merchant Navy if I wear glasses?+

Yes, in most cases, as long as your corrected vision meets the required standard. Very high power (severe myopia) may cause issues.

Can I join Merchant Navy if I have flat feet?+

Depends on severity — mild flat feet is usually fine, but a medical examiner will assess mobility and fitness during the exam.

Can asthma patients join Merchant Navy?+

Generally not accepted, especially if it's an ongoing/chronic condition, due to fitness requirements for physically demanding and remote work conditions.

Can diabetic candidates join Merchant Navy?+

Insulin-dependent diabetes is usually disqualifying. Mild, diet-controlled cases may be evaluated case-by-case by the medical examiner.

Is there an age limit to join Merchant Navy?+

Yes, typically 17-25 years for cadet-level entry, varying by course and institute. Some lateral-entry or GP Rating courses may allow slightly different limits.

Can girls apply for the same medical standards as boys?+

Yes, medical standards are largely the same for all genders, with fitness and health being the main criteria, not gender-specific limits.

What eye tests are done during the medical exam?+

Visual acuity test, color vision test (Ishihara test), and sometimes night vision assessment — done at DG Shipping-approved hospitals.

Is hearing test part of the medical exam?+

Yes, normal hearing is required since clear communication is critical for safety onboard; hearing aids are generally not accepted for sea duty.

Can candidates with tattoos join Merchant Navy?+

Tattoos aren't typically disqualifying by DG Shipping rules, but some private companies may have their own policies — best to check with your specific institute/company.

Is a dental checkup part of the medical exam?+

Yes, basic dental health is checked as part of the overall medical fitness exam.

Can candidates with heart conditions join?+

No, any significant cardiac condition usually disqualifies candidates due to safety risks in remote sea environments with limited medical access.

Do I need to redo medical exams every year?+

Yes, seafarers need periodic medical renewal (usually every 2 years, or as required) to keep sailing, done at approved hospitals.

Can candidates with a history of surgery join Merchant Navy?+

Depends on the type and recovery status of the surgery — a medical examiner will assess if it affects long-term fitness for sea duty.

What happens if I fail the medical exam?+

You may be disqualified for that specific course/rank, though some conditions allow retesting after treatment, or you can explore alternate non-sea-going maritime roles.

How many years does the Merchant Navy course take?+

DNS: 1 year pre-sea training plus sea time. B.Tech Marine: 4 years including sea time. GP Rating: 6 months to 1 year.

What is pre-sea training?+

Classroom and practical training before your first ship — covers navigation/engine basics, safety courses (STCW), and physical training. Usually residential/hostel-based.

How is hostel life during training?+

Disciplined, similar to defense-style training — fixed routines, uniforms, physical training, and strict rules. Prepares you for ship life.

What subjects are taught during pre-sea training?+

Navigation/engineering basics, ship construction, safety procedures, first aid, seamanship, and physical fitness, depending on Deck or Engine stream.

Is pre-sea training tough?+

It's disciplined and demanding — early mornings, physical drills, and academics together — but manageable with the right mindset.

What is onboard training/sea time?+

After pre-sea training, cadets join a ship for practical training under senior officers, gaining real sailing experience needed to sit for further exams.

How long is the onboard training period?+

Usually 6-18 months depending on the course and company requirements, before returning for further studies/exams.

What is STCW training and is it part of the course?+

Yes, STCW (fire fighting, sea survival, first aid, personal safety) is a mandatory part of pre-sea training, required before joining any ship.

Do all institutes provide the same quality of training?+

No, quality varies — always check DG Shipping approval, faculty experience, ship-visit/simulator facilities, and past placement records before choosing.

Are simulators used during training?+

Yes, good institutes use navigation and engine room simulators to give practical exposure before actual sea time.

What is the uniform/dress code during training?+

Most institutes have a fixed uniform (similar to defense academies) worn during classes, drills, and parades.

Is there physical training (PT) during the course?+

Yes, daily physical training and fitness drills are a standard part of pre-sea training at most institutes.

Can I take a break between pre-sea training and sea time?+

Not usually recommended — most institutes/companies expect cadets to join a ship soon after training completion to keep certifications and placement on track.

What exams are given after sea time?+

Officer competency exams (like 2nd Mate/4th Engineer exams) conducted by DG Shipping, required to move up in rank.

Is there a difference in training duration for Deck vs Engine cadets?+

Pre-sea duration is similar (around 1 year for DNS/GME), but sea time and rank exam patterns differ slightly between the two streams.

Do I get a stipend during onboard training?+

Yes, most companies pay a training stipend/salary to cadets during their sea time, though the amount varies by company.

What happens if I fail an exam during training?+

You usually get a chance to reappear after additional preparation, though repeated failures can delay your progression to the next rank.

Is hostel accommodation compulsory during training?+

Yes, most institutes require residential/hostel stay during pre-sea training for discipline and access to training facilities.

Can I pursue higher studies after Merchant Navy training?+

Yes, many officers later pursue higher marine qualifications, MBA, or shore-based professional courses using their sea experience.

How is training different from a regular college degree?+

It combines strict discipline, physical training, safety courses, and hands-on ship training — much more practical and regimented than a typical classroom-only degree.

Is placement guaranteed after the Merchant Navy course?+

No course can legally "guarantee" placement — be cautious of institutes claiming 100% guarantee. Good institutes have strong placement records and tie-ups, but final selection depends on interviews and performance.

What is the salary after the course?+

Cadets start around ₹25,000-40,000/month; officers earn significantly more with experience — Captains/Chief Engineers can earn ₹8-15 lakh+ per month depending on ship type and company.

How do I get a job after course completion?+

Through campus placements, manning agents/RPSL-licensed agencies, or direct company hiring — your institute usually helps arrange interviews after training completion.

Which companies hire freshers?+

Major companies include Anglo-Eastern, V.Ships, Fleet Management, Bernhard Schulte, Maersk, and Synergy — hiring usually happens through campus placements or manning agents.

What is the placement process like?+

Typically: resume shortlisting, aptitude/technical test, personal interview, medical clearance, then offer letter and joining formalities.

Do all institutes offer campus placement?+

No, only reputed and well-connected institutes have strong campus placement tie-ups — always check placement history before taking admission.

What is an RPSL license?+

Recruitment and Placement Service License — issued by DG Shipping to authorize manning agents to legally recruit seafarers for shipping companies.

How soon after training do I get my first ship?+

It varies — good candidates from reputed institutes with strong placement cells may get a ship within a few months; others may take longer depending on market demand.

Is there a difference in placement for Deck vs Engine cadets?+

Demand fluctuates by year and company, but overall both streams have similar placement opportunities with reputed shipping companies.

Do foreign shipping companies hire Indian cadets?+

Yes, Indian officers and cadets are in high demand globally due to strong training standards and English proficiency.

What is a contract period on a ship?+

Usually 4-9 months depending on rank and company policy, followed by paid leave before the next contract/ship.

Is the salary the same across all shipping companies?+

No, it varies based on company reputation, ship type (tanker, container, bulk carrier), rank, and nationality of the crewing company.

Do tanker ships pay more than other ships?+

Generally yes, tanker vessels (oil/gas/chemical) tend to pay higher due to additional risk and specialized training required.

What happens if I don't get placed after training?+

You can keep applying through manning agents, attend more interviews, or in some cases pursue additional certifications to improve chances.

Is there scope for growth within a shipping company?+

Yes, sticking with one reputed company often helps faster promotions, better ships, and priority contracts due to established track record.

Can I switch shipping companies later in my career?+

Yes, officers commonly switch companies for better pay, ship type, or contract terms once they have sufficient experience and clean record.

Do shore jobs exist after a Merchant Navy career?+

Yes, experienced officers can move into shore roles like marine surveyors, superintendents, maritime trainers, or port operations.

Are placement chances better with government or private institutes?+

Both can offer good placement if reputed — government institutes are often more affordable, while some private ones have strong direct company tie-ups.

What documents are needed at the time of placement/joining a ship?+

CDC, passport, valid medical certificate, STCW certificates, and visa (if required) — company will confirm the full checklist before joining.

What is a CDC and how do I apply?+

CDC (Continuous Discharge Certificate) is your sea service record book, issued by DG Shipping. Applied for after admission, through your institute or RPSL agent, along with medical and other documents.

How long does it take to get a CDC?+

Usually a few weeks after submitting complete documents, but can vary based on processing and verification.

What is a Seaman Book and why is it needed?+

It's an official ID for seafarers, required to board ships and travel internationally for sea duty. Often used interchangeably with CDC in India.

How to apply for a passport for Merchant Navy candidates?+

Apply through normal or Tatkal passport service. Merchant Navy candidates often get priority processing once admission/joining documents are shown.

What is an INDoS number?+

A unique ID issued by DG Shipping to every Indian seafarer, required before CDC application. Registered online via the DG Shipping portal.

What is a PCC (Police Clearance Certificate)?+

Needed for visa and CDC processes to confirm no criminal record. Applied through local police station/passport office.

What medical exams are needed before joining a ship?+

A pre-employment medical exam (PEME) at a DG Shipping-approved hospital — checks eyesight, hearing, fitness, and overall health.

Which hospitals are approved for Merchant Navy medical tests?+

Only DG Shipping-approved hospitals are valid — the list is available on the DG Shipping website; using a non-approved hospital means the certificate won't be accepted.

Is Yellow Fever vaccination mandatory?+

Yes, if sailing to certain countries/regions where it's required by international health regulations.

What documents are needed at college admission?+

10th/12th marksheets, ID proof, medical fitness certificate, passport (or in process), and category/income certificates if applicable.

What documents are needed at the time of joining a ship?+

CDC, passport, visa (if required), medical certificate, STCW certificates, and company-specific joining documents.

What is STCW certification?+

Mandatory international safety training (fire fighting, first aid, sea survival, etc.) required before joining any ship.

How to renew CDC or Seaman Book if it expires?+

Apply for renewal through DG Shipping with updated medical certificate and required fees, before it expires to avoid delays in joining.

Do I need a visa to join a ship?+

Yes, depending on the port of joining — usually arranged/assisted by the shipping company or manning agent.

What happens if documents are incomplete at joining time?+

Joining can get delayed or cancelled — always double-check documents (CDC, passport, medical, STCW) well before your joining date.

What is a NOC in Merchant Navy?+

A No Objection Certificate, sometimes required when switching companies or for specific administrative/visa purposes.

What certificates are mandatory for foreign-going ships?+

STCW basic safety courses, CDC, valid medical certificate, and rank-specific certificates of competency (for officers).

What is a GDS (General Declaration of Seafarer) or crew list document?+

A document listing crew details submitted at ports for immigration and customs purposes, handled by the ship's officers, not individually by cadets.

Do I need travel insurance to join a ship?+

Companies typically provide insurance coverage for crew, but some also recommend/require personal travel insurance for the joining journey.

What is a flag state endorsement on CDC/certificates?+

An official validation confirming your certificates meet the standards of the country whose flag the ship sails under — required for certain international voyages.

Is Merchant Navy a risky or dangerous profession?+

Not as risky as movies show. Ships follow strict international safety rules, and crew get mandatory safety training before sailing. Piracy has also dropped a lot due to naval patrols and security measures. It's demanding, not reckless.

Can girls join Merchant Navy?+

Yes, and many are already Captains and Chief Engineers. Same salary, same growth as men. Main challenge is fewer women in senior ranks right now, but that's changing fast.

Is it a 24/7 no-rest job?+

No fixed weekends, but rest hours are legally mandated (minimum 10 hrs/24 hrs under MLC rules). It's shift-based, not rest-less. After each contract, you get 2-4 months fully paid leave.

Do sailors get internet on ships?+

Most modern ships have satellite internet now, though slower and limited compared to shore. Older ships may have little to none. Improving every year.

Is Merchant Navy salary really high?+

Yes, especially compared to shore jobs at the same experience level — tax-free income too (for NRIs). But it varies a lot by rank, company, and ship type.

Can a fresher become Captain directly?+

No. You start as a Cadet, then move up through ranks (2nd Officer, Chief Officer, Captain) based on sea experience and exams. Takes 8-10+ years typically.

Does Merchant Navy affect health long-term?+

Long time away from family and irregular sleep can affect mental health if not managed. Physical health risks are low with proper safety compliance and regular medical checks.

Do sailors lose contact with family for months?+

Not fully — internet/calling is available on most ships now, though limited. Contracts run 4-9 months, but you get long paid leave after each one.

Is Merchant Navy stressful?+

Can be — long contracts, isolation, and responsibility add pressure. But good time management and support systems onboard help a lot.

Is there no future after 40 in Merchant Navy?+

Myth. Many officers move into senior sea ranks, shore-based jobs (marine surveyors, superintendents, maritime teaching) or shift ashore with their sea experience valued highly.

Can commerce/arts students join Merchant Navy?+

Deck cadet courses (like DNS) usually need PCM in 12th. But non-technical roles (like GP Rating, catering, purser) are open to other streams too.

Is there an age limit to join?+

Yes, usually 25 for cadet-level entry (varies by course/company), but exceptions exist for certain courses and lateral entries.

Do sailors get seasick their whole career?+

No — most people adjust within the first few weeks/months at sea. Seasickness is common initially, rare long-term.

Can you lose your CDC/license for a small mistake?+

Only for serious violations (like DG Shipping rule breaches, misconduct, safety violations) — not for minor errors. It's a strict process, not arbitrary.

Do companies blacklist cadets for leaving mid-contract?+

Leaving without proper reason/process can hurt your record and future hiring. Always follow proper sign-off procedure through the company.

Is it true you can't switch back to a shore job after Merchant Navy?+

False. Many former sailors move into logistics, shipping companies, maritime training, or other shore-based roles using their experience.

Do Merchant Navy officers get more respect than shore jobs?+

Respect varies by perception, but the profession is globally recognized and well-paying, with a strong career structure.

Are placement guarantees by institutes genuine?+

Be cautious — always verify the institute's track record, DG Shipping approval, and past placement data before trusting "100% guaranteed" claims.

Do sailors see different countries or just stay on the ship?+

Depends on port stay duration — sometimes there's time to step out, sometimes ships turn around quickly. It's a mix, not guaranteed sightseeing.

Is engine cadet job "dirtier" than deck cadet?+

Engine roles do involve more machinery/oil work, while deck involves more navigation/watch-keeping. Neither is "lesser" — different skill sets, similar pay scale. Merchant Navy: Practical Scenario Q&A (Part 3) Documents, Eligibility, Admission + Extra Training/Placement/Myths Documents — Specific Requirement Q&A

What documents are required to enroll in an STCW course?+

10th/12th marksheet, ID proof (Aadhaar/passport), passport-size photos, and sometimes your institute admission letter — check with the STCW training center directly as it can vary slightly.

How many documents need to be submitted to get a CDC made?+

Typically: INDoS registration proof, medical fitness certificate, passport copy, PCC, photographs, and institute/company sponsorship letter — usually 6-8 documents in total.

Is a Merchant Navy admission letter required to apply for a passport?+

Not compulsory, but it helps speed up processing and priority handling at the passport office if you mention it's for sea duty.

Is an original marksheet needed to get a Seaman Book made?+

Usually a copy is fine along with other ID documents, but carry originals for verification — requirements vary slightly by RPSL agent/DG office.

What documents need to be uploaded for an INDoS number?+

Photo, signature, 10th marksheet (for DOB proof), and address proof — done online via the DG Shipping INDoS portal.

What other documents do I need to carry along with medical tests at the hospital?+

ID proof, passport-size photos, and sometimes an admission/sponsorship letter from your institute or company.

Which address proof is accepted for a Police Clearance Certificate (PCC)?+

Aadhaar card, voter ID, or utility bill are commonly accepted — same as a normal police verification process.

What documents does the company ask for when applying for a visa?+

Passport, CDC, medical certificate, and sometimes an employment/joining letter from the company — visa process is usually company-assisted.

Do I need to carry original documents when joining a ship, or do photocopies work?+

Yes, originals are usually required at the time of joining for verification, though you should also carry photocopies as backup.

If my STCW certificate is lost, how do I get a duplicate made?+

Contact the training institute that issued it — they can usually reissue a duplicate certificate for a small fee with ID proof. Eligibility — Specific "Can I Apply" Q&A

Can shorter candidates apply for Deck Cadet, or only Engine side?+

Height requirement (usually 150-157 cm) applies to both Deck and Engine roles similarly — it's not Engine-only; check the specific institute's minimum height criteria.

If height is below 150 cm, what roles are available?+

Very few officer-level roles may be affected, but it's best to get a medical pre-check done, as some institutes are stricter than others — GP Rating criteria may differ too.

Do underweight candidates get rejected during the medical exam?+

Being significantly underweight (low BMI) can cause issues in the medical exam — gaining healthy weight before applying is advisable.

If my glasses power is high (-6/-7), can I still become a Deck Cadet?+

Very high power may not meet the required limits for Deck — Engine side is generally more lenient on eyesight power, but always confirm with a DG-approved hospital.

If one eye is weak and the other is normal, can I still get selected?+

Possible in some cases if the combined/corrected vision meets standards, but severe imbalance between both eyes can be disqualifying — needs proper testing.

If I had asthma as a child but I'm fine now, can I still apply?+

Yes, if there's no current/active condition and your medical report shows full recovery — the examiner will assess your current lung function, not childhood history alone.

Does having a visible tattoo on the body cause a problem during the medical exam?+

No, tattoos aren't a medical disqualification by DG Shipping rules, though some private shipping companies may have their own appearance policies.

If I have flat feet, which department (Deck/Engine) has fewer restrictions?+

Neither Deck nor Engine strictly disqualifies mild flat feet — it depends on the severity assessed during the medical exam, not the department.

If I'm overweight, how much weight do I need to lose before selection?+

Enough to bring your BMI into a healthy range (roughly 18.5-25) — there's no fixed number, it depends on your height and current medical evaluation.

Can left-handed candidates join Merchant Navy, or is there any restriction?+

Yes, there's no restriction on left-handedness for any Merchant Navy role. Admission — Specific Scenario Q&A

If I did 12th with Commerce, which Merchant Navy courses can I still apply for?+

GP Rating and other non-PCM crew-level courses are open to you; Deck/Engine officer courses (DNS, GME, B.Tech Marine) require PCM in 12th.

If I get a low rank in IMU CET, what are my chances of getting into a decent college?+

You may still get into a private/less competitive institute through counseling, but top government institutes usually need a higher rank — check previous year cutoffs.

Is it safe to take admission through the management quota?+

It can be safe if the institute is DG Shipping-approved with good placement history — always verify credentials directly, don't rely only on agents' claims.

If I cancel my admission once, can I take admission in another college the same year?+

Depends on the institute's policy and counseling round timelines — some allow it if seats are still open, others may not refund/reallot easily.

Can I take admission if my 12th result is still awaited (compartment/pending subject)?+

Usually no — most institutes require a cleared 12th result at the time of final admission confirmation, though provisional applications may be allowed initially.

If I already have a B.Sc/B.Com degree, do I still need to give IMU CET like a fresher?+

Yes, IMU CET (or equivalent) is generally still required for Merchant Navy courses regardless of your prior degree, unless a specific lateral-entry scheme applies.

Can I apply to a Merchant Navy course in a different state than my home state?+

Yes, there's no state-domicile restriction for most Merchant Navy institutes — you can apply anywhere in India based on your IMU CET rank/preference.

If my category certificate (SC/ST/OBC) is not ready yet, can I still apply provisionally?+

Many institutes allow provisional admission with a declaration, but you'll need to submit the actual certificate within a set deadline to retain quota benefits.

If I miss the IMU CET exam date, is there any other way to get admission that year?+

Some private institutes offer direct admission without IMU CET, or you may need to wait for the next IMU CET cycle (usually held twice a year).

Does having a backlog/failed subject in 12th affect Merchant Navy admission eligibility?+

Yes, most institutes require a cleared 12th (no pending backlog) at the time of final admission — a compartment exam must be cleared before confirmation. Training — Extra Scenario Q&A

Are mobile phones allowed during pre-sea training?+

Usually restricted to specific hours or weekends only — most institutes maintain discipline similar to a boarding school/academy.

Is swimming compulsory during training?+

Yes, basic swimming ability is often required or taught as part of sea survival training — check with your institute if you can't swim yet.

If I fail a subject during pre-sea training, can I still go for sea time?+

No, you typically need to clear all pre-sea exams before being sent for onboard training/sea time.

Can I request a specific ship type during onboard training?+

Not usually — ship assignment is based on company/vacancy availability, not personal preference, especially for first-time cadets.

Is ragging an issue during training?+

Reputed institutes have strict anti-ragging policies; always choose a well-reviewed, regulated institute to avoid such issues. Placement — Extra Scenario Q&A

If I don't get placed through my institute, can I apply directly to companies?+

Yes, you can directly approach RPSL-licensed manning agents or shipping companies even if your institute doesn't arrange placement.

If my first ship experience is bad, can I request a change of company next time?+

Yes, once your contract ends, you're free to apply to a different company for your next contract.

Does having lower marks in pre-sea training affect placement chances?+

It can matter for top companies, but strong performance in interviews and physical fitness often weigh more for entry-level placement.

If I get seasick badly on my first ship, will I be sent back?+
